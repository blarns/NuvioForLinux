Third-party component: TorrServer
=================================

This application bundles the TorrServer binary to provide optional
peer-to-peer (P2P) torrent streaming on Linux.

  Project:  TorrServer
  Upstream: https://github.com/YouROK/TorrServer
  Version:  MatriX.141.5
  License:  GNU General Public License v3.0 (see TorrServer-LICENSE.txt)

TorrServer is bundled UNMODIFIED as a separate, standalone executable. Nuvio
does not link against it: it is launched as a local child process and driven
over localhost HTTP. This constitutes "mere aggregation" under the GPL, so the
GPL does not extend to Nuvio's own code.
